# Offside — SME-118

Fixtures now enforce offside using IFAB 2026/27 Laws 11 and 13. A position alone never produces a whistle. The game freezes eligibility when a teammate touches the ball, then checks whether an eligible player becomes actively involved. An offence stops play for an indirect free kick and, in a player-controlled fixture, pauses for an explanation. Inspect players freely, then press Enter or Continue to acknowledge the call. The awarded taker then uses the existing restart controls.

## Position and timing

`OffsidePhase` is an immutable snapshot with a play ID, touch tick, toucher, ball position, attacking direction, opponent line and eligible attackers. It considers every opponent, including the keeper; it does not assume that the keeper is last. The second-last opponent and the forward edge of the ball both matter, as does the attacking half. Being level is onside. A player's later return towards their own goal does not erase their earlier eligibility, and a player who was onside at the touch may run beyond opponents before receiving.

`OffsideBody` supplies an envelope of playable body parts. The current placeholder capsules use a 0.3-metre longitudinal radius; arms and hands do not contribute. Animated skeleton-based body samples are outside this placeholder prototype. The ball uses the fixture's 0.11-metre radius. These geometry samples, rather than attributes or a random draw, determine eligibility.

Open-play passes, shots, headers, crosses, receptions, controlled touches and keeper distributions record phases. The model's release is the contact point for a kick and the final hand contact for a keeper throw. Receipt is a first-touch boundary before a new possession/reception prompt. Continuous controlled dribbling updates the touch snapshot as the simulation advances. Practice-only worlds retain their existing behavior; `StartFixture` enables the rules.

## Involvement and intervening touches

`OffsideTracker.Evaluate` requires actual involvement. Its table-tested evidence types distinguish playing the ball, challenging an opponent, obstructing vision, attempting to play with a clear effect, and an obvious action with a clear effect. Opponent-impact evidence must identify an opponent from the phase and affirm a clear impact.

The live model checks contact before awarding possession or a reception decision. It also checks active receivers/chasing players contesting a reachable ball with a nearby opponent, and a playable capsule obstructing a keeper's sightline during an on-target shot in the goalmouth. Nearby position alone is insufficient. These deterministic geometry thresholds model the limited movements available in the prototype; richer gestures, collisions and referee judgement can provide additional evidence later.

Every new teammate touch creates a new snapshot. Deliberate opponent control/play replaces the old phase, even if the ensuing pass or clearance fails. Reflex blocks and keeper deflections preserve it; deliberate saves also preserve it. Keeper parry integration is tested through the actual save application and rebound receipt. Neutral goal-frame or match-official rebound evidence is supported and tested by the tracker; those physical events are not generated by the current world (post/crossbar collisions and referee bodies remain unmodelled). Any stoppage ends the phase.

Direct goal kicks, throws and corners create exempt phases. The next teammate touch restores ordinary offside checking. Kickoffs and indirect free kicks do not receive those exemptions. Exemption and touch history are independent of the passing success roll.

## Whistle and restart

An offence records an `Offside` fixture event and retains `LastOffside`, including the original snapshot, involvement location/tick, evidence kind, rebound reason and explanation. The whistle clears pending actions and cancels the current flight. Offside contacts do not increment completed-pass counts. Calling for the same receipt again cannot create another whistle after the phase has ended; the existing action commit caches still prevent replays. No additional probability draw is used for the rule.

The opposing team gets an indirect free kick at the involvement location, which can be in the offender's own half. An attacking indirect free kick inside the opponents' goal area is moved to the goal-area line as required. Opponents are placed at least 9.15 metres away and, for a defending kick inside its penalty area, outside that area. A boundary-safe positioning fallback prevents clamping from undoing the required distance. The current restart delivery uses the shared pass model and cannot score directly: an untouched opponent-goal entry becomes a goal kick, and an untouched own-goal entry becomes a corner.

Full foul/advantage ordering, cards, deliberate leaving/re-entering the field, specialist free-kick routines and referee animations remain later work. Players currently stay within the simulated pitch except for legal restart placement.
